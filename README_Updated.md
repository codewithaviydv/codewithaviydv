## 👋 About Me

# Abhjeet Kumar
### Diploma Civil Engineer | Site Engineer

I am a **Diploma Civil Engineer** with **3 years of hands-on site experience** in construction execution, drawing interpretation, material management, and quality supervision. I coordinate labour teams and contractors, monitor work progress against schedules, and help ensure that site activities meet approved drawing specifications and safety standards.

My additional **ITI (Electrician)** training gives me a practical understanding of site electrical works. I am seeking a **Site Civil Engineer** role with a growth-oriented construction or infrastructure company.

**Location:** Gorakhpur, Uttar Pradesh, India
