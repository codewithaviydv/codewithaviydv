### Software Engineer | B.Tech in Computer Science & Engineering

I am **Abhjeet Kumar**, a Computer Science and Engineering graduate with a multidisciplinary engineering background. I completed my **B.Tech in Computer Science and Engineering** from **Dr. A. P. J.
